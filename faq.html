The basics

What is Learn to Read, Read to Learn?
It's an interactive reading app for phone, iPad and web that teaches reading the way great teachers do — then goes further. It combines machine learning that listens as you read, proven reading-teaching methods, and a patient companion called Mr. Swindell.
Who is Mr. Swindell?
Mr. Swindell is your in-app companion, guide, mentor and teacher in one — a character with a name, a face and a memory. He knows your name, remembers yesterday's chapter, chats about the story, explains words, makes up new tales about things you love, and celebrates every win. He's a friendly character in the app, not a real person.
Is it free?
It's free to start — no reading level required. Finding your level is Mr. Swindell's job.
What devices can I use?
Phone, iPad and web, with one account so your progress follows you everywhere — phone in the queue, iPad on the couch, web at school.

How it teaches

How does the app teach reading?
Three kinds of intelligence, one patient teacher: machine learning that listens as you read aloud, evidence-based reading pedagogy (systematic phonics, decodable texts, fluency and comprehension), and Mr. Swindell walking beside you on every page.
How does it listen to my reading?
Speech recognition hears every word you read aloud, spots exactly where decoding breaks down, and adapts the next page to you — the right challenge, never a wall, never busywork.
What are the two phases — 'learn to read' and 'read to learn'?
Phase one, learning to read, cracks the code with phonics lessons that adapt to what the app hears and decodable stories matched to the sounds you know. Phase two, reading to learn, uses that code — Mr. Swindell discusses stories, builds vocabulary and comprehension inside real texts, and generates new stories around your interests. The app is built for both sides of that pivot.
Do I need to know my reading level to start?
No. Start on any device in under a minute; Mr. Swindell listens to one short passage, finds your level, and builds your first reading path with you.

Who it's for & accessibility

What ages is it for?
Any age. Young readers, adult learners, and English-language learners each get their own path and tone — the app grows with you across reading's great pivot.
Is it suitable for dyslexic readers?
Yes. It's readable by design: hyperlegible type, adjustable size and spacing, and dyslexia-friendly options throughout.
Can adults and English-language learners use it?
Absolutely. It's built for learners of any age and starting point, with a path and tone suited to adult learners and English-language learners.
How does progress tracking work?
Clear dashboards for learners, whānau and teachers show accuracy, fluency and books finished — progress you can see.

For parents & safety

Is it safe for my child?
Child safety is our priority. The app focuses solely on reading, is private by default, and stores data on EU-resident infrastructure under strong child-privacy rules. For anything about your specific child or account, our support assistant will connect you with a person.
What data do you collect about my child?
We keep data to the minimum needed to teach reading and show progress, on your child's own account. We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't use a child's content to train external AI models. See the privacy policy for the full detail, or ask us to connect you with a person.
Can teachers and schools use it?
Yes. Teachers get their own dashboards showing accuracy, fluency and books finished, alongside the learner and whānau views.
Can I talk to a real person?
Yes. This assistant answers common questions about the app, and can connect you with a member of our team for anything about a specific child, account or safeguarding concern.
