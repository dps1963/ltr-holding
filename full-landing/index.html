How it works

Three kinds of intelligence, one patient teacher.

The app combines machine learning, evidence-based reading pedagogy, and Mr. Swindell — a mentor with a name, a face and a memory, so learning to read feels like time with a favourite teacher, not time with software.

Machine learning that listens

Speech recognition hears every word you read aloud, spots exactly where decoding breaks down, and adapts the next page to you — the right challenge, never a wall, never busywork.

the app learns you while you learn to read

Proven teaching methods

Built on the science of reading: systematic phonics, decodable texts, fluency practice and comprehension strategies, sequenced the way reading specialists teach them — not gamification with a book skin.

pedagogy first, technology in service of it

Meet Mr. Swindell

Your companion, guide, mentor and teacher in one. He knows your name, remembers yesterday's chapter, chats about the story, answers "what does that word mean?", spins up new tales about whatever you love, and celebrates every win — endlessly patient, never judging.

a teacher's warmth, always in your pocket

Why the name

The app grows with you across reading's great pivot.

Every reader crosses a line where reading stops being the subject and becomes the tool for everything else. The app is built for both sides of that line — and for learners of any age crossing it.
